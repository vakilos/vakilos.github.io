Apps for musicians.

Simple, reliable tools for rehearsals and the stage. I make Melos, an app for keeping songs, setlists and scores together and playing from them on stage, and its companion for preparing songs on a bigger screen.

Apps

Melos

Android · songs, setlists and Live mode

Everything for a song in one place, and a calm Live mode for the stage.

  • Lyrics with chords, notes with song sections and pedal cues
  • PDF scores and lyrics pages you can write on, audio and backing tracks
  • Setlists and a Live mode you swipe through, or turn with a Bluetooth footswitch
  • Works offline; nothing is sent to any server. Optional sync through your own Google Drive.
Coming soon to Google Play

Melos Companion

Web, Windows, macOS, Linux · free

Prepare songs and setlists on a laptop or tablet, then send them to Melos on the phone. Paste a chord sheet from the web and turn it into chords in one click; attach PDFs and audio; build setlists. It runs in the browser and keeps everything on your computer.
